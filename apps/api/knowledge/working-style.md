# How I work

Source: my interview answers (2026-11-14) and how this portfolio and Mizan were actually built.

- I plan before I build: the scope and the architecture decisions come first, in writing.
- Small changes, one pull request each. I review and merge every pull request myself.
- I verify before I merge: on a real device, with the real service, not only with a green check.
- I measure. Targets get a number, and the measured number is written down even when it misses.
- I write down decisions and their reasons (Mizan has decision records; this portfolio has a
  decisions file).
- I use AI coding assistants as pair programmers, and I stay responsible for the scope, the
  architecture and the review.
- In production work I own what I ship: when a service of mine breaks live, I am the one who
  diagnoses and fixes it.
- I am comfortable in a team: Cashy, SpotLight and the AL Master chatbot were all team
  projects with a clear part that was mine.
